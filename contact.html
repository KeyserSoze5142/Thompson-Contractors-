FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers about our services, service area, and how to get started on your project.

What areas does Thompson Contractors serve?

Thompson Contractors, LLC is based in Tuscaloosa, Alabama and serves Tuscaloosa, Northport, Birmingham, Vestavia Hills, Mountain Brook, Hoover, Homewood, Helena, Pelham, Chelsea, Huntsville, Madison, Smith Lake, and surrounding Alabama communities.

What services does Thompson Contractors offer?

We offer custom home building, luxury kitchen remodeling, bathroom remodeling, home additions, custom covered patios and outdoor living spaces, and full design services including 2D and 3D renderings before construction begins.

Is Thompson Contractors licensed and insured?

Yes. Thompson Contractors, LLC is a licensed Alabama general contractor (AL License #27707) and a member of the Home Builders Association of Alabama (HBAA).

How do I get an estimate for my project?

Contact us by phone at (205) 210-9897, email Info@ThompsonContractorsLLC.com, or use our contact form. Share a brief description of your project along with any photos or drawings, and we will follow up to discuss your vision, budget, and timeline.

Do you provide design services before construction?

Yes. Our team of designers, draftsmen, and structural engineers creates 2D and 3D renderings for custom homes, kitchen and bathroom remodels, and major additions, so you can see your project clearly before construction begins.

How long does a custom home or remodel take?

Every project is different. During the planning phase we provide a detailed schedule, and our client portal gives you real-time updates so you always know exactly where your project stands.
