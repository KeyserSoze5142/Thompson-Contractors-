Meet Colby Thompson

Founder & Owner — born and raised in Tuscaloosa, building Alabama homes on a foundation of integrity, craftsmanship, and service.

The Story

Hometown Roots. Higher Standards.

Colby Thompson is the founder and owner of Thompson Contractors, LLC. Born and raised in Tuscaloosa, he discovered his passion for entrepreneurship and hands-on work at a young age — and founded the company to elevate industry standards by combining personalized client care with meticulous attention to detail.

“Success means lifting others along the way.”

At Thompson Contractors, family, entrepreneurship, and mentorship are at the core of the business. Away from the job site, Colby enjoys the outdoors with his wife Gabriella and their two sons, Knox and Miller.

Beyond the Build

Invested in the Next Generation

Colby is passionate about mentoring the next generation of builders and entrepreneurs, and regularly sponsors local events and youth initiatives across the Tuscaloosa community.
